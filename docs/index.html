Sync bookmarks across AI browsers & Chrome — directly in your Google Drive.

FavoSync is an open-source Chromium extension built for Ego Lite, Chrome, Edge, and Brave. No proprietary cloud, no browser account lock-in — your bookmarks stay 100% in your own Google Drive.

View on GitHub Privacy Policy

Zero Browser Account Lock-in

Works even in browsers without built-in Google account sync (such as Ego Lite) using standard OAuth 2.0 PKCE.

Smart 3-Way Merge

Delete a bookmark on Computer A and add a new one on Computer B — SyncFav merges both cleanly without duplicates.

Failsafe & Local Backups

Automatically blocks accidental mass deletions and keeps 10 local restore points before applying remote updates.

Minimal Permission Scope

Uses Google Drive's drive.file scope only — SyncFav can only access its own SyncFav/bookmarks.json file.
