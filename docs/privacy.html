Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Overview

SyncFav is a browser extension that synchronizes your browser bookmarks across devices using your own Google Drive account. We do not operate a database storing your bookmarks, nor do we sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

2. Data Accessed & Stored

  • Browser Bookmarks: Read and written locally via the browser's bookmarks API strictly for synchronization and local backup.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): SyncFav creates and updates only its own folder and file (default: SyncFav/bookmarks.json) in your Google Drive. It cannot read, modify, or delete any other files in your Drive.
  • Google Email Address (userinfo.email): Displayed inside the extension UI so you can verify which Google account is connected.
  • Local Storage: OAuth tokens, sync preferences, logs, and local bookmark backups are stored exclusively on your device via chrome.storage.local.

3. Data Transfer & OAuth Proxy

Bookmark content is transmitted directly between your browser and Google Drive over HTTPS. When using the public OAuth flow, only OAuth authorization codes and refresh tokens are exchanged through a stateless Cloudflare Worker proxy to protect the OAuth client secret; no tokens or bookmarks are logged or persisted on the proxy.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

SyncFav's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from SyncFav Settings, revoke access from your Google Account Permissions page, and delete the SyncFav folder from your Google Drive.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the SyncFav GitHub repository.
